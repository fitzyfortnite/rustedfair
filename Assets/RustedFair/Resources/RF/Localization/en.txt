note_body_5 = That tape is the old opening announcement. When it plays, every machine in this place comes running to the booth — it's how they were programmed to greet the crowd. I used it to lure them away once. You just used it on yourself. Hide.

note_body_6 = I froze this note so Brutus couldn't tear it apart. He's the big one. Slow, but he never stops and he checks the lockers. Oil won't trip him — he's too heavy. Use the mask, or run until he loses you.

note_body_7 = At midnight the doll comes out to say goodnight. She never says it to me anymore. If you have all seven digits, go to the Security Office and open the safe. The gate alarm will wake everything that's left. Don't stop running until you feel the rain.
